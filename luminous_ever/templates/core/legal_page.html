Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Luminous Ever collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you visit our website, contact us, request a proposal, use a service or otherwise interact with us.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it is provided. Where Indian data-protection law applies, we process personal data in accordance with the applicable legal requirements, including applicable provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules as and when they apply to the relevant processing.

Plain-English promise.We do not sell your personal information. We use information to communicate with you, operate the website, deliver services, protect our systems and improve our work.
02

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Name, business name and contact details when you submit an enquiry or request a proposal.
  • Email address, phone number and communication details when you contact us.
  • Project requirements, budgets, preferences, files, website details and other information you voluntarily provide.
  • Information required to administer an active client relationship, including project communications and approvals.

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser/device information, approximate location derived from technical data, pages visited, referring pages and basic usage information.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used for essential functionality, analytics or marketing where those technologies are enabled.
  • Campaign and referral information, including UTM parameters, where present.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through ordinary website enquiry forms. Please do not submit passwords, financial account credentials, government identity numbers or other sensitive information through a public form unless we specifically request it through an appropriate secure process.

03

How we use information

Depending on the context, information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate about requested services.
  • Prepare proposals, estimates, scopes and project documentation.
  • Deliver, maintain and support websites, marketing, SEO, design, development and other agreed services.
  • Process and administer payments, invoices and contractual records where applicable.
  • Measure website performance, understand how visitors use our website and improve user experience.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, investigate incidents and enforce our terms.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, accounting or dispute-resolution obligations.
  • Send service-related communications. Promotional communications will be sent only where permitted and with the choices required by applicable law.
04

Sharing & service providers

We may share information only when reasonably necessary to operate the business, deliver a service, comply with law or protect legitimate interests. This can include trusted providers for hosting, email, analytics, communications, payment processing, cloud storage, development infrastructure and security.

Where a client asks us to work with a third-party platform—such as Google, Meta, a CRM, advertising platform, hosting provider or analytics service—we may need to transmit or access relevant project information through that platform. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, court order, governmental authority, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety or property of Luminous Ever, our clients or others.

We do not sell personal information as a business model.

05

Retention & security

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain legitimate business records, complete a client relationship, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or meet legal/accounting obligations.

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. No internet transmission, storage system or security control can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

When information is no longer reasonably required and there is no legal reason to retain it, we may delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it.

06

Your choices & rights

Depending on applicable law and your relationship with us, you may have rights relating to access to personal data, correction, updating, withdrawal of consent, deletion/erasure and grievance or complaint mechanisms.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will handle valid requests within the period required by applicable law.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that was lawfully completed before withdrawal, and it may not affect processing that we are permitted or required to carry out on another lawful basis.

07

Cookies & analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential operation, preferences, analytics, security and marketing. Analytics or advertising tools are enabled only where configured for the website.

Our current application can be configured with Google Analytics and Meta Pixel. If those tools are enabled, they may collect technical and usage information according to their own policies. You can also control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality.

08

Children

Our website and professional services are intended for businesses and general audiences, not for children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through our website.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, data practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated through an appropriate channel where required.

01

Refund policy

Luminous Ever provides professional digital services that are often customised, scheduled and performed specifically for a client. This policy explains how cancellations and refunds are handled for recurring services and one-time projects.

Simple rule.You are charged for work that has been performed or committed, while genuinely unearned prepaid fees may be refundable where this policy, your project agreement or applicable law requires it.
02

Monthly subscriptions & retainers

Recurring plans are billed for the agreed service period. A cancellation request should be made at least 7 days before the next billing date unless your signed agreement specifies a different notice period.

  • Work already performed during the current billing period is not refundable.
  • Committed third-party costs and non-cancellable expenses are not refundable.
  • If we receive a cancellation before the next billing period begins, the next period will not normally be charged.
  • Partial-month refunds are not normally provided after a recurring period has started, except where expressly agreed or required by applicable law.
03

One-time projects

For websites, branding, development, SEO setup and other fixed-scope projects, the applicable proposal or statement of work determines the deposit and milestone structure.

  • Deposits cover project reservation, discovery, planning, resource allocation and work performed after approval.
  • Once work has started, the amount attributable to work completed and committed project costs is non-refundable.
  • If a project is cancelled before any substantive work begins, we will assess the amount of genuinely unearned fees and refund that amount where appropriate, after deducting any agreed non-refundable or already-incurred costs.
  • Where a project-specific agreement contains a different cancellation/refund arrangement, that agreement controls.
04

When a refund may be due

A refund may be appropriate where:

  • We have received money for a service that we have not started and have no committed non-refundable cost for, subject to the applicable agreement.
  • We agree in writing to refund an amount as part of a cancellation or resolution.
  • A refund is required by applicable consumer or other law.
  • We are unable to deliver an agreed service and the applicable agreement provides for a refund.

Refund eligibility is assessed against the actual scope, work completed, payment history, third-party commitments and the applicable agreement.

05

Amounts that are generally non-refundable

  • Work already completed or delivered.
  • Approved change requests or additional work already performed.
  • Third-party costs that have already been paid or cannot be cancelled.
  • Advertising spend already committed or spent on a client's behalf.
  • Premium software, licences, domains, hosting or other external purchases made specifically for the project where the provider does not permit cancellation/refund.
  • Amounts expressly identified as non-refundable in an accepted project agreement.
06

How to request a cancellation or refund

Send the request through our Contact page or the contact email published on this website. Include your name/business name, invoice or project reference if available, the service involved, the requested cancellation date and the reason for the request.

We may ask for reasonable information needed to verify the request. We will review the request against the applicable agreement and this policy and communicate the decision.

07

Refund timing

Where a refund is approved, we will initiate it using the original payment method where reasonably possible. The time taken for the amount to appear in your account can depend on the payment provider or bank.

We will not intentionally delay an approved refund beyond the period required by applicable law or the agreed payment terms.

08

Mandatory legal rights

This policy does not remove or restrict any right, remedy or refund entitlement that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited. If applicable consumer-protection, contract or other law gives you a mandatory right, that right will apply even if this policy says otherwise.

Nothing here is intended to create a waiver of statutory rights.
