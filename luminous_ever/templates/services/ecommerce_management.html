What we manage

Account management means more than uploading products.

Our workflow connects merchandising, marketplace operations, performance marketing and commercial reporting instead of treating each task as a separate service.

01

Catalog & listing operations

New SKU creation, bulk uploads, attribute mapping, category placement, titles, bullets/descriptions, search terms, variations, images, size charts, content QA and listing-error resolution.

02

Listing optimisation

Improve discoverability and conversion through structured titles, relevant search terms, clear value propositions, compliant attributes, imagery and content testing where platform tools permit.

03

Ads & marketplace visibility

Campaign setup, product selection, budget allocation, bid/budget monitoring, search-term analysis and performance reviews. Advertising spend remains client-funded and is not included in management fees unless stated.

04

Inventory & order operations

Stock availability checks, low-stock flags, order monitoring, fulfilment exceptions, cancellation trends and coordination with the client’s warehouse or fulfilment partner.

05

Returns, claims & seller support

Return reason tracking, issue logging, support tickets, dispute documentation and escalation follow-up. Outcomes depend on platform policy and evidence available.

06

Settlement & reconciliation

Order-level reconciliation support, fee/commission checks, deductions, returns and payout variance tracking, with escalation of identified discrepancies to the platform or finance team.

07

Pricing, promotions & sale events

Competitor and margin-aware pricing reviews, promotion planning and marketplace event readiness. Final commercial pricing and discounts remain subject to client approval.

08

Account health & compliance

Monitor available performance indicators, listing issues, policy warnings and operational risks. We do not guarantee reinstatement or approval; we prepare evidence and follow the platform process.

09

Reporting & growth planning

Weekly operating updates and monthly performance reviews covering sales, orders, conversion, advertising, returns, inventory and actionable next steps.
